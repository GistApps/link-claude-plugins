# Gist CRM

Use Gist CRM with Claude to prepare for customer conversations, keep
records current and review your sales pipeline. Claude can pull a customer's
full history, find who an email belongs to, summarize deals by stage and log
notes, calls and follow-up tasks after a meeting.

## Use it

1. Add the plugin, then connect **Gist CRM** from the plugin's Connectors
   tab and sign in with your GistLink account.
2. Choose the organization Claude may act in. The connection only reaches
   CRM, with your own permissions.
3. Ask things like "Brief me on Acme before my 2pm call" or "Log that I spoke
   with Dana and set a follow-up for Friday."

Skills included:

- **account-brief**: a one-page brief on a contact or company with deals,
  recent activity and open tasks.
- **log-interaction**: records a call or meeting, updates the deal and adds
  follow-up tasks.

## Data

The plugin sends your requests and the CRM records you work with to your
GistLink account through `https://mcp.gist.link/crm/mcp`. It stores nothing
itself. See the [privacy policy](https://gist.link/privacy) and the
[connector documentation](https://developers.gist.link/mcp/connectors/crm/).
