# Gist Calendar

Plan your time with Claude using Gist Calendar. Get a briefing on today or
the week ahead, find a time when everyone is free, and create, move or cancel
events, including recurring ones. Claude checks with you before inviting
anyone or cancelling an event.

## Use it

1. Add the plugin, then connect **Gist Calendar** from the plugin's
   Connectors tab and sign in with your GistLink account.
2. Choose the organization Claude may act in. The connection only reaches
   Calendar, with your own calendars and permissions.
3. Ask things like "What's on my calendar tomorrow?" or "Find 30 minutes with
   Sam and Priya next week."

Skills included:

- **daily-agenda**: summarizes a day or week with conflicts and gaps.
- **schedule-meeting**: finds shared free time, proposes slots and creates the
  event once you pick one.

## Data

The plugin sends your requests and the events you work with to your GistLink
account through `https://mcp.gist.link/calendar/mcp`. Attendees you add receive
invitations from Gist Calendar. The plugin stores nothing itself. See the
[privacy policy](https://gist.link/privacy) and the
[connector documentation](https://developers.gist.link/mcp/connectors/calendar/).
