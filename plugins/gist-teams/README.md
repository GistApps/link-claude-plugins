# Gist Teams

Stay on top of your team's work with Claude and Gist Teams. Get a digest of
what you missed in channels and direct messages, search past discussions, post
updates, and turn requests into cards on your task boards with owners and due
dates.

## Use it

1. Add the plugin, then connect **Gist Teams** from the plugin's
   Connectors tab and sign in with your GistLink account.
2. Choose the organization Claude may act in. The connection only reaches
   Teams, with your own memberships and permissions.
3. Ask things like "Catch me up on #launch" or "Make a card for Sam to update
   the pricing page by Friday."

Skills included:

- **catch-up**: summarizes unread activity, mentions and cards assigned to you.
- **create-task**: turns a request into a card in the right board column.

## Data

The plugin sends your requests and the messages, boards and cards you work
with to your GistLink account through `https://mcp.gist.link/teams/mcp`.
Messages you choose to send are visible to the channel or conversation. The
plugin stores nothing itself. See the [privacy policy](https://gist.link/privacy)
and the [connector documentation](https://developers.gist.link/mcp/connectors/teams/).
