# Gist Support

Help your support team move faster with Claude and Gist Support. Get a
prioritized view of the queue, summarize long ticket conversations, draft
replies for review and keep status, assignment and tags tidy. Public replies
reach customers, so Claude asks before sending one.

## Use it

1. Add the plugin, then connect **Gist Support** from the plugin's
   Connectors tab and sign in with your GistLink account.
2. Choose the organization Claude may act in. The connection only reaches
   Support, with your own agent permissions.
3. Ask things like "What's urgent in the queue?" or "Draft a reply to ticket
   4821."

Skills included:

- **triage-queue**: ranks open tickets by urgency and SLA risk and suggests
  owners.
- **draft-ticket-reply**: summarizes a ticket and drafts a reply or internal
  note for approval.

## Data

The plugin sends your requests and the tickets you work with to your GistLink
account through `https://mcp.gist.link/support/mcp`. Public replies you approve
are delivered to the requester by Gist Support. The plugin stores nothing
itself. See the [privacy policy](https://gist.link/privacy) and the
[connector documentation](https://developers.gist.link/mcp/connectors/support/).
